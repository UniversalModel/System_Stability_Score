## ★ What TRA delivers — the useful core (read this first)

Strip the caveats for one page and state plainly what this appendix *gives you*. TRA is a **unifying diagnostic language for rocket stability** — one map that sits above the specialist toolchain and makes a single question legible across all of it: *of structure, trajectory, and thrust, which is the floor right now?* Six concrete deliverables, useful on day one:

1. **One map for the whole vehicle.** Everything that flies a rocket — airframe, tanks, engine, guidance, trajectory, propellant — sorted into three bags: **Form** (the geometry that must persist), **Position** (the trajectory-and-environment it is in), **Action** (the thrust and control it exerts). One shared language for the structures engineer, the trajectory analyst, and the propulsion team.

2. **The non-compensation principle — a teachable law of flight.** `U = ∛(F·P·A)` says a rocket *multiplies* its virtues; it does not average them. A dead pillar is unrecoverable: full thrust cannot save a buckled airframe; a perfect airframe cannot save a spent tank on a lost trajectory. This is the **reusable-rocket truth** — the landing burn is hard precisely because all three pillars sit near their floor at once. It is the single most transferable idea in the appendix, and it is *correct as stated.*

3. **The stability matrix — an engineering artifact, not a slogan.** §7 places **every rocket detail** on the F/P/A grid, each with **the operational theory that actually governs it** (shell-buckling knockdown factors, Bartz heat flux, Fay–Riddell stagnation heating, Rubin POGO transfer matrices, G-FOLD, PRA) and its **cross-pillar couplings**. It is a genuine cross-index of rocket failure modes and the disciplines that own them — a review checklist and a teaching tool with zero validation required.

4. **The weakest-pillar triage — a cross-cutting attention router.** `π* = argmin(F,P,A)` names which of the three holds the current bottleneck, in one currency, across incommensurable failure classes. It computes no physics; it tells a design review *which specialist gets the next question.*

5. **The center–periphery phase profile — a design-review lens.** §12 shows the binding pillar *migrating* through the flight: Action at liftoff, Form at max-Q, Form-and-Position on entry, Action-and-Position on the landing burn. A compact answer to *which pillar owns the margin in which regime.*

6. **A responsible dual-use boundary.** Because a launch vehicle and a missile share the physics, §5 draws a hard, teachable line — own-vehicle, safety-of-flight, stabilize-only — worth stating before any of the above is validated.

**That is the contribution, and it stands on its own** — a language, a map, a principle, a triage, and an ethic; a research and design-review instrument. ## ★★ Measurement & meaning — what the F/P/A numbers actually are (the calibration foundation)

For the map to carry a *number* rather than a mood, the three scores need one shared meaning — otherwise `U` and `π*` could drift under an arbitrary rescale. TRA therefore fixes **one cardinal semantics for all three pillars**, up front:

> **Each pillar score is the calibrated probability that its requirement is met over a stated flight horizon:** `F` = P(structure/geometry holds through the phase), `P` = P(state stays inside the survivable corridor), `A` = P(thrust + control + propellant suffice) — each estimated against a fixed reference distribution, reported on `[0,1]` with an uncertainty interval.

Under this contract the three numbers are **commensurable** (three probabilities on one scale), `U = ∛(F·P·A)` is a **normalized geometric-mean score** — a *monotone index* of the joint survival probability `F·P·A` (exact only under independence), **not itself a probability** (the honest survival probability is `P_surv = P(F∩P∩A)`, and `U = P_surv^{1/3}` only when the pillars are independent; the §11 GSM carries the dependence), and `π*` is **rescale-stable** — a probability is not free to be monotonically re-scaled, so the weakest-pillar address does not move under cosmetic transforms. Row-to-pillar aggregation (non-compensatory for loss-of-vehicle rows, §11.1), transformations, missing-data behavior, and provenance are **frozen before any test** — the *measurement contract* — so a pass certifies the object everyone actually scores. This is what turns the map from a metaphor into a measurable, and it is the first thing a real program would pin down.

## 0. What TRA is, what it is NOT, and what is new

TRA introduces **no new science, no new aggregator, no new runtime.** Aerodynamics, structural mechanics, orbital mechanics, combustion, and GNC are untouched and unreplaced; each keeps its own validated method and produces its own ground truth. TRA's single contribution is a **registration** plus its falsifiers: it proposes that the state of a launch-and-land vehicle factors into three separable bags (Form / Position / Action), scores each flight-state node with the inherited keystone `U = ∛(F·P·A)`, reads the weakest pillar `π* = argmin(F,P,A)` as a *candidate* address of the driving failure mode — **and then tries to refute all of that (§B).**

**TRA IS:**
- a **proposed decomposition** of a flight vehicle's state into Form (geometry/structure/mass), Position (gravitational-atmospheric context + point on trajectory), Action (thrust/control/combustion/feedback);
- a **score** — `U = ∛(F·P·A)`, non-compensatory **at the zero limit**, so a truly collapsed pillar collapses survival and is always visible (in the interior it is a penalized trade, §2);
- a **weakest-pillar diagnostic** — `π* = argmin(F,P,A)`, a *hypothesized* bottleneck/failure-mode address, gated by TRA-P1/P2;
- a **profile integral** — `ℳ = ∫U dt` over the flight = a *proposed* accumulated survival margin. **Caveat (load-bearing): the integral can mask a brief fatal state** — 99 s at 0.99 plus 1 s at 0.01-with-vehicle-loss still integrates high. So the objective is **lexicographic: hard specialist constraints first, then `U_min = min_t U(t)` and time-below-threshold, and only then the integral** — the ascent and the propulsive landing are two `U(t)` curves that must stay above threshold **through every phase**, which is *not* the same as a high average.

**TRA IS NOT (the non-negotiables):**
1. **NOT a replacement for aerospace engineering.** The specialist (CFD, FEA, trajectory optimizer, combustion model, flight software) produces ground truth; TRA *indexes and routes* it. Permitted verbs: **decompose / score / localize / route.** Forbidden: *replace / supersede / control.*
2. **NOT validated.** No comparison to flight telemetry has been run here. Architecture is B1 (true of the design); every "U tracks survival / the weak pillar is the failure mode" sentence is **B3-pending**, gated by §B.
3. **NOT in the loop.** TRA is a *post-hoc / design-review diagnostic over abstract or logged states*, never a controller issuing commands to a real vehicle. Human-on-top is structural.
4. **NOT a weapons tool.** See the firewall (§5). This is the load-bearing constraint of *this* domain, because rocketry's dual-use is the same equations.

## 1. The triad registered onto an ascending-and-landing vehicle

Each pillar is a score on `[0,1]` (SSS convention) plus its native representation. **These assignments are proposals under test (TRA-P0), not settled taxonomy** — several items are flagged as cross-pillar confounds.

### 1.1 FORM (F ↔ Time) — the geometry that must persist

| Form sub-object | What it is | Collapse = |
|---|---|---|
| **Airframe / hull geometry** | fineness ratio, outer mold line, aerodynamic shape | structural buckling; loss of aeroshape |
| **Nozzle geometry** | bell/aerospike, throat + expansion ratio, contour | throat erosion; flow separation; burn-through |
| **Moving-part geometry** | gimbal joints, grid fins, actuators, valves, turbopump rotor | actuator jam; fin failure; pump geometry loss |
| **Internal volumes** | tank shape, ullage, feed plumbing, COPVs, interstage voids | tank collapse/over-pressure; plumbing rupture |
| **Fluid-path form** | injector pattern, flow-path geometry, boundary layer, shock/expansion structure | flow maldistribution; unstable shock geometry |
| **Mass geometry** | CG location, moments of inertia, bending-mode shapes | CG excursion; adverse structural-mode coupling |

> The **time-varying** nature of Form is essential and is why Form binds to **Time**: as propellant drains, CG and bending modes migrate through the burn. Thermal-protection geometry and staging-interface geometry belong here too.

### 1.2 POSITION (P ↔ Space) — the context the vehicle is *in*

| Position sub-object | What it is | Collapse = |
|---|---|---|
| **Gravitational context** | the masses along the trajectory (Earth / Moon / multi-body), the local `g`-field, the orbital-mechanics regime | wrong regime for the target — the orbit you are *on* misses the orbit you *need* (the ΔV shortfall itself is **Action**, §1.3) |
| **Atmospheric medium** | density/pressure layers, dynamic pressure `Q`, max-Q, aeroheating, winds/shear — *the suitability of the medium at this altitude* | exceed structural `Q` limit; thermal environment beyond TPS; wind-shear upset |
| **Trajectory point / attitude** | the flight phase (liftoff, ascent, max-Q, staging, coast, entry, landing burn, touchdown) and attitude vs. velocity vector (angle of attack) | outside the corridor; adverse angle of attack; off-nominal state at a phase boundary |
| **Static-margin geometry (F×P confound)** | the center-of-pressure–to-center-of-gravity relation (static margin) | **negative static margin** — aerodynamic attitude stability is lost and must be carried *entirely by Action* (TVC); see the confound note below |
| **Landing-site context** | terrain, pad/approach corridor, ground effect | no viable footprint; approach outside the reachable set |

> **Position energy-language audit (minor-2):** all five Position rows describe *geometric / regime state* (where and in what field the vehicle is), never energy delivery. ΔV, thrust energy, and propellant are **Action**. Static margin is listed here but flagged: it is intrinsically a **Form × Position** coupling (a geometry, CP–CG, evaluated in an aerodynamic context) and is one reason the pillars are **not symmetric** — a §B.0 confound, not a clean Position item.

### 1.3 ACTION (A ↔ Energy) — thrust, control, and the release of energy

| Action sub-object | What it is | Collapse = |
|---|---|---|
| **Propulsion / combustion** | reactive thrust, chamber pressure, mixture ratio, `Isp`, **combustion-acoustic stability** | engine-out; combustion instability; hard start |
| **Control-system action** | GNC (guidance/navigation/control), thrust-vector control, reaction-control thrusters | loss of control authority; TVC saturation |
| **Feedback / reactions** | closed sensor→actuator loops, stability augmentation, **closed-loop gain/phase margin** | sensor loss; loop instability; latency-driven divergence |
| **Capacity & fuel** | propellant budget, throttle authority, ΔV / landing-fuel margin | propellant depletion; insufficient ΔV; no landing margin |
| **Human action** | crew inputs, abort decisions | mis-input; missed abort window |
| **Ground action** | mission-control computers/systems, range safety, tracking, uplink | command-link loss; ground-segment failure |

> **Named cross-pillar confounds (minor-10, and the core of §B.0).** Several of the harshest rocket instabilities are **simultaneously Form × Position × Action** and cannot be filed to one bag:
> - **POGO** (structure ↔ feed ↔ thrust oscillation) — Form (tank/feed geometry) × Action (combustion/feed dynamics);
> - **propellant slosh** — Form (tank geometry/baffles) × Action (control loop) × Position (acceleration state);
> - **aeroservoelastic flutter / control-structure (bending-mode) interaction** — Form (modes) × Position (dynamic pressure) × Action (control loop);
> - **combustion-acoustic instability** — filed to Action, but the acoustic modes are *set by chamber/injector geometry* and the fixes (baffles/resonators) are *geometric*, so it double-loads **Form**;
> - **closed-loop gain/phase margin** and the **pitch/yaw stability margin** — an **emergent F × P × A product**, not an Action-only "loop instability."
>
> **These are the strongest known threats to separability, and they are named here, not buried.** The §B.0 perturbation-leakage metric is designed to *register* them; the honest expectation is that they set the ceiling on how separable this domain really is.

## 2. The keystone — `U = ∛(F·P·A)` as a *proposed* survival index, with non-compensation restricted to the zero limit

`U = ∛(F·P·A)` is **proposed** (not asserted) as a loss-of-vehicle survival index. The one qualitative claim held as robust (B1) is the **zero-limit** behavior, which on a rocket is not a metaphor:

- **F → 0** (structure/geometry truly lost): the vehicle breaks up; no thrust or trajectory reassembles it.
- **P → 0** (no survivable trajectory state remains): loss, even with intact structure and full thrust.
- **A → 0** (thrust *and* control *and* propellant lost): loss, even with a perfect airframe on an ideal path.

`U = ∛(F·P·A)` sends the aggregate to zero when any factor does, and its gradient is largest at the smallest pillar — the formal shadow of the engineer's "driving failure mode."

> **Non-compensation is a ZERO-LIMIT property, not an interior one (minor-1).** Away from zero the geometric mean **is** compensatory: `F=0.5, P=1, A=1 → U=0.79`. So `U` **permits penalized trades in the interior and does not model a hard threshold constraint** (a `Q`-limit, a minimum static margin, a minimum landing-fuel reserve). Where flight safety is a *hard* constraint, `U` is the wrong object and the specialist's constraint governs; `U`'s claim is confined to (a) the zero limit and (b) a *proposed* monotone association with survival margin that TRA-P1 must earn.

- **Imbalance** `δ = (max−min)/(max+0.01)`; **imbalance-penalized survival readout** `SI = U/(1+δ)²` (legacy label 'stability index' — it carries ZERO dynamical-stability content and **double-penalizes dispersion**, since the geometric mean already penalizes imbalance; REVIEW-v2 M5). **v1.1 status (minor-9):** `δ` and `SI` are **communication aids with ZERO current evidentiary role** unless and until TRA-P1b (pre-registered) shows they add held-out predictive value over `U` alone. They may not absorb a null post hoc.
- **Threshold `φ⁻¹ = 0.618` (minor-3):** an **inherited SSS placeholder with NO physical meaning for flight.** It is *not* a calibrated soft-landing survival threshold; any flight threshold must be **fit or discarded under TRA-P1**, and until then no numeric threshold is claimed.
- **Mission integral** `ℳ = ∫U dt` = a *proposed* accumulated survival margin; `𝒮 = ∫(1−U) dt`; canon fixes `ℳ + 𝒮 = T`.

**Ascent and propulsive landing as two `U(t)` curves.** Ascent stresses Position hardest at **max-Q** and stacks all three at **staging**; **peak structural and thermal (Form) loads occur at ascent max-Q and at entry, NOT at the landing burn (minor-4).** The **propulsive landing** is the hard curve for a different reason: it is **Action-margin-bound** (near-depleted propellant → thin ΔV/throttle authority) and **Position-corridor-bound** (a violent, narrow entry/landing regime), with Form loads already past their peak. A "soft" landing is, on the hypothesis, a trajectory that keeps the *proposed* `U(t)` above whatever threshold TRA-P1 calibrates, all the way to touchdown, while `δ` stays bounded.

> **Boxed law (§2, one line, v1.1).** *`U=∛(F·P·A)` is **proposed** as a rocket's survival index: at the zero limit it is genuinely non-compensatory — structure, trajectory, or thrust gone to zero ends the flight and no surplus recovers it — while in the interior it is only a penalized trade, not a hard constraint; the weakest pillar is a **candidate** failure-mode address, and a mission is the integral `ℳ=∫U dt` — all of it gated by TRA-P0/P1/P2, none of it asserted as measured.*

### B.0 — TRA-P0: separability, made operationally well-posed (v1.1)

- **Claim:** a vehicle's state factors into F/P/A whose scores move independently under a perturbation of their *own* inputs, **net of the admitted through-burn coupling.**
- **H0:** the F/P/A assignment explains no more pillar-separable variance than a random three-way partition of the same telemetry/feature set.
- **Named confounds to defeat (the strongest threats, not a give-away):** propellant drain (Action→Form CG/modes, Action→Position reachable set); **POGO; slosh; aeroservoelasticity / control-structure interaction; static margin (F×P); combustion-acoustic instability (A/F); closed-loop gain/phase & pitch-yaw stability margin (F×P×A).**
- **Conditioning set (pre-registered):** the **primary estimator is conditional distance-correlation (dCor), bounded `[0,1]`**; CMI is admissible only if renormalized to `[0,1]` via `√(1−e^(−2I))` (REVIEW-v2 M3). **No Pearson |r|.** Computed conditioned on a **FROZEN** set (not 'at least'): {propellant-mass fraction, `Q`, CG offset, MOI, first-bending-mode frequency, slosh-mass fraction} — propellant-fraction alone is an incomplete sufficient statistic for drain coupling (REVIEW-v2 M2). **AND** the **UNCONDITIONED** leakage `Λ_raw` is reported with its own pre-registered kill, because TRA-P1 and `ℳ=∫U dt` consume the *unconditioned* `U(t)`: a conditional-only pass does NOT license unconditional `U`.
- **Intervention protocol (pre-registered):** instantaneous thrust/control perturbation at **fixed propellant state**, or drain-residualized inputs, evaluated on **held-out** flights.
- **Leakage estimator:** cross-pillar leakage `Λ ∈ [0,1]` = the held-out **partial-distance-correlation** of the *other two* pillar scores on the perturbed pillar's induced change, normalized to `[0,1]` (defined HERE, not deferred — REVIEW-v2 M8). Default tolerance `τ_sep = 0.15`; the 'most-separable' cross-domain margin is fixed at **`m = 0.10`** on the separability score, with a **byte-identical `Λ` estimator required across siblings** before any rank is admissible (REVIEW-v2 M9).
- **PASS (explicit conjunction):** `Λ < τ_sep` **AND** conditional dependence below the 0.6–0.8 band on held-out data **AND** the measured partition beats the random-partition null. The **0.6–0.8 band defaults to NOT-a-pass** (inconclusive ≠ pass).

## 11. GSM — modelling the F×P×A coupling instead of pretending it away (uncertainty-aware `U`; borrowed from TRC §2.9 via the TE β-channel)

The §7.4 cross-pillar rows (POGO, slosh, aeroservoelasticity, combustion-acoustic, static / gain-phase margin) are exactly where "three separable bags" is false and **the failure IS the coupling** — no pillar is near zero, so a point `U` reads high right up to divergence. TRA does not pretend these away; it borrows the **Gaussian Stability Matrix (GSM)** from `APPENDIX_TRC §2.9`:

- Stack the pillar/phase log-odds into `z ∈ ℝⁿ`, `μ` mean, `Σ = D·R·D`. The **off-diagonal correlation blocks `R`** are the F/P/A dependence — the same `ρ_FPA` TRC-P0 measures — **estimated, never assumed**, ingested from the **TRA-P0 perturbation-leakage battery** (§B.0): the measured leakage `Λ` *informs* the off-diagonal via a **stated, pre-registered monotone mapping `ρ = m(Λ)`** — a **modelling choice, NOT an identity** (`Λ` is a non-negative distance-dependence; `ρ` a *signed* Gaussian correlation, so the sign and calibration of `m` are declared, never assumed). `R` is shrunk to a PSD estimate (Ledoit–Wolf / OAS); and **when dependence is under-determined, the decision-use rule is bounds / fail-closed, not a point estimate** — shrink-to-independence is used only for visualization, since it can *under*-state common-cause risk.
- `U` is then **not a point but a credible interval reflecting the modelled dependence** — positive coupling generally widens it and drops the lower tail. POGO / slosh / flutter therefore no longer read as a false-high point `U`; they read as a **wide, low-confidence `U`**. This is **epistemic uncertainty under a stated covariance assumption — a risk-visualization, NOT an instability detection or a stability certificate**: a wide interval says the *model is unsure*, not that divergence is proven.
- Every off-diagonal carries `G{source; test; date}` provenance (§10 discipline); a `Σ` with *assumed* off-diagonals is `axis_unseparated` and renders no interval, only the `Σ→0` point fallback.
- **Risk** = a **model-based failure-probability estimate** under the stated GSM assumptions (an unvalidated model output, **not** a validated posterior) against the (unvalidated, stakes-scaled) threshold; **GSM-Priority = E[ΔP_fail | intervene on cell] / ∛(C_time·C_space·C_energy)** — a *min / posterior* object, not a mean.

### 11.1 Non-compensatory within-pillar aggregation (resolves REVIEW-v2 M1)

The §7 grid holds ~12 rows per pillar. **A weighted mean is wrong for loss-of-vehicle (LOV) rows:** a single common-bulkhead rupture, COPV burst (AMOS-6), weld-defect tank rupture, single-engine RUD, or throat burn-through zeros **one** row while the rest stay healthy — a mean keeps the pillar ≈0.9 and `U` stays high, so the very catastrophes §2 cites as the zero-limit are **not reproduced by the appendix's own aggregator.** **v1.3 rule:** LOV-class rows aggregate **non-compensatorily** — the pillar takes a **hard per-row floor / min over the LOV set** (one dead LOV row drives its pillar → 0), while non-LOV rows may mean-aggregate. This makes "the zero-limit is always visible" **true of the aggregator**, not merely of an idealized fully-collapsed pillar. The LOV-row set is the pre-registered FMEA / FTA catastrophic set, not chosen post hoc.

### 11.2 GSM in practice — a runnable reference, real numbers, and the "GSM-lite" recipe

GSM is not just prose: **`tra_gsm_reference.py`** (shipped with this appendix) implements it in ~30 lines of NumPy and runs in a second. Two results make the mechanism concrete (illustrative pillar inputs; the U-distribution math is exact):

That is the physics of coupled failure the scalar hides.

**The distributional gate flags the right phase.** Scoring a Starship-class phase profile against the rule *"pass iff `P(U ≥ 0.75) ≥ 0.90`"* — this answers the "how do you threshold a distribution" question: you gate the **probability of exceedance**, not a scalar:

## 13. Coherence — the Kuramoto layer for multi-engine and combustion synchrony (the NDT 5th-currency analogue, made runnable)

A 33-engine cluster (Starship-class), combustion-acoustic modes, and structural modes are **coupled oscillators**: they can lock in phase — desirable thrust synchrony, or destructive POGO / screech. The **Kuramoto order parameter `r ∈ [0,1]`** is the *measurable* coherence quantity (the classical analogue TRA marks provisional against NDT's quantum-reserved 5th currency). **`tra_kuramoto_reference.py`** (shipped) computes it.

**Coherence onset.** For N=33 oscillators with unit-std natural-frequency spread the critical coupling is `K_c = 2√(2/π) ≈ 1.60`; below it the cluster is incoherent; above it, it locks:

| K | r | state |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 0.30 | incoherent |
| 1.6 (`K_c`) | 0.68 | partial |
| 3.0 | 0.89 | LOCKED |
| 4.0 | 0.95 | LOCKED |

A **detuned "rogue" engine** (1 of 33 far off-frequency) stays out of the lock at K=1.6 (r=0.53) but is pulled in by K=3.0 (r=0.92) — a concrete read on whether a mis-tuned engine desynchronizes the cluster or is entrained.

**The §11↔§13 bridge — coherence *drives* the coupling.** The elegant unification: the Kuramoto `r` feeds the GSM off-diagonal `ρ_FPA` (`ρ ≈ ρ_max · r`). As engines / modes synchronize (`r` rises), cross-pillar coupling rises, the GSM `U`-interval widens and its lower tail drops (§11.2). **Rising coherence is rising risk, computed end-to-end** — a phase model → a correlation → a survival-index distribution.
